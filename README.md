# The Princess Diaries: A Journey from an Ordinary Girl to a Princess

## A. Introduction

**The Princess Diaries** is a 2001 coming-of-age comedy film produced by Walt Disney Pictures and directed by Garry Marshall. It follows the life of Mia Thermopolis, an ordinary and socially awkward high school student whose life completely changes when she discovers that she is the princess of Genovia. The movie tells a heartwarming story about self-discovery, family, friendship, confidence, and learning to accept unexpected changes in life.

- **Genre:** Coming-of-Age, Comedy, Family, Romance
- **Release Year:** 2001
- **Director:** Garry Marshall
- **Production Company:** Walt Disney Pictures
- **Based on:** The Princess Diaries novel by Meg Cabot

## B. Synopsis

The Princess Diaries follows Mia Thermopolis, a shy and socially awkward teenager living in San Francisco with her mother. Like many other high school students, Mia struggles with school life, friendships, and fitting in with the people around her. Her ordinary life takes an unexpected turn when she discovers that her late father was the Crown Prince of Genovia, making her the heir to the throne. Her grandmother, Queen Clarisse Renaldi, visits her and introduces her to the responsibilities and expectations of being a princess. Mia must learn proper royal etiquette, improve her confidence, and adjust to a completely different lifestyle while still trying to experience a normal teenage life. However, she begins to question whether she is truly meant to become a princess and whether she can handle the responsibilities that come with her royal identity. Throughout her journey, Mia learns valuable lessons about accepting herself, standing up for what she believes in, and understanding that being a princess is more than just looking and acting like royalty.

## C. Characters

Here are five important characters in the movie:

1. **Mia Thermopolis** – The main character of the movie. She is a shy and awkward high school student who discovers that she is the princess and heir to the throne of Genovia.

2. **Queen Clarisse Renaldi** – Mia's grandmother and the Queen of Genovia. She teaches Mia about royal responsibilities, proper etiquette, and how to carry herself as a princess.

3. **Lilly Moscovitz** – Mia's best friend who is outspoken, confident, and supportive. She stays by Mia's side even when her life changes after discovering her royal identity.

4. **Joe** – Queen Clarisse's head of security and trusted driver. He becomes a fatherly figure to Mia and helps guide and protect her throughout her journey.

5. **Michael Moscovitz** – Lilly's older brother who is a kind and talented musician. He develops romantic feelings for Mia and appreciates her for who she really is.

## D. Personal Review

I chose **The Princess Diaries** because it has been one of my favorite childhood movies. I have always found it charming, heartwarming, and entertaining. I especially love how the movie portrays Mia's journey from being an ordinary high school student who feels almost invisible to discovering that she is actually a princess.

I also love Anne Hathaway's performance as Mia because her acting makes the character adorable, relatable, and memorable. Another thing I really appreciate is the chemistry between Anne Hathaway and Julie Andrews. Their interactions as granddaughter and grandmother make the movie even more enjoyable and meaningful.

### Three Things I Liked About the Movie

- **Mia's Character Development:** I love watching Mia grow throughout the movie. She starts as a shy teenager who struggles with her confidence, but gradually learns to accept herself and become more comfortable with her identity.

- **The Chemistry Between the Actors:** Anne Hathaway and Julie Andrews have wonderful chemistry. Their interactions make the relationship between Mia and Queen Clarisse feel genuine, funny, and heartwarming.

- **The Coming-of-Age Story:** I like how the movie shows that growing up involves discovering who you are, making difficult decisions, and learning to accept changes. Mia's experience reminds me that we do not have to be perfect to become someone we can be proud of.
